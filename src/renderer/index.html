A note on GSAP before you start

GSAP is the animation library behind most of the sites that make people say "how did they do that." Since April 2025 it is completely free, including every plugin that used to cost money, and the licence covers paid client work. Load it from a CDN in two lines and you are done.

<script src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/gsap@3/dist/gsap.min.js"></script>
<script src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/gsap@3/dist/ScrollTrigger.min.js"></script>

The previews here are built in plain CSS so they always run, even offline. The prompts ask for GSAP, which is smoother and far easier to sequence once you have more than one thing moving.

Let's get you set up

This takes about five minutes, and you only do it once. Two things: a free key so the app can find businesses, and a few details about you so it can write emails that sound like you.

Nothing you do here leaves this computer. Your leads, your notes and your key are stored on this machine. There is no ArturaLabs server holding them.
You will not be charged by us for searches. You use your own free account, and you can see exactly how many searches you have left inside the app.
You can change any of this later from Your Details and API Keys in the sidebar.

Get your free search key

This is what lets the app pull local businesses. It is free to start and does not ask for a card.

  1. Click the button below. SerpApi opens in your browser.
  2. Sign up with an email address. No card needed.
  3. On your dashboard, find Your Private API Key and copy the whole thing.
  4. Come back here and paste it in.
